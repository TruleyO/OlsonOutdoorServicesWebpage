An Olson Family Story

The Work Came First. The Lessons Stayed.

A connection to the outdoors passed from father to son, and carried forward into a business of its own.

Where It All Began

A Childhood Spent Learning the Trade.

My introduction to landscaping wasn't something that began in a classroom or with a business plan. It started when I was a kid, going to work with my mom and dad.

At the time, my parents couldn't afford babysitting, so it made more sense for me to accompany them to job sites. What began simply as a family making ends meet eventually became something much more meaningful.

As I grew older, I became more involved in the work. I watched, listened, helped where I could, and gradually learned what my dad called the "tricks of the trade."

Those early experiences gave me a foundation in outdoor work that has stayed with me throughout my life.

A Generation Before OOS

Lyle Olson Landscapes

My father, Lyle Olson, operated his own landscaping business. It was alongside him that I received my earliest hands-on introduction to the trade.

Carrying the Lessons Forward

A New Business. Deeply Rooted Values.

My dad passed away in 2019, but the lessons he taught me continue to influence the way I approach my work today.

Olson Outdoor Services LLC is not an inherited company or a continuation of Lyle Olson Landscapes as a business. It is something I'm building independently, shaped by the knowledge and experiences that came before it.

To me, carrying that legacy forward doesn't mean trying to recreate the past. It means taking what I learned, putting it to work, and continuing to grow through the opportunities ahead.

The name may be different, and the business may be new, but the foundation behind it reaches back to those childhood days spent working alongside my father.
