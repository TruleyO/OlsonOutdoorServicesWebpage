Service Category 01

Lawn & Landscape Care

Attractive, well-maintained grounds help create a positive first impression. Our lawn and landscape services focus on consistent care, attention to detail, and maintaining the appearance of your outdoor spaces.

Lawn Care

Lawn Mowing & Trimming

Keep your lawn looking neat and professionally maintained with routine mowing and string trimming.

Depending on the agreed service scope, visits may also include minor litter and small debris pickup before mowing, along with blowing grass clippings from sidewalks, curbs, and other maintained hard surfaces.

Landscape Care

Landscape Bed & Light Landscape Maintenance

Maintain the appearance of existing landscape beds with hand-weeding, bed cleanup, and general planting-bed care.

Services can also include landscape bed top dressing, small plant replacements, and other light maintenance of established landscapes, as agreed in the estimate.

Shrub Care

Shrub & Hedge Trimming

Thoughtful trimming and shaping help maintain the appearance of established shrubs and hedges throughout the growing season.

OOS approaches shrub trimming with attention to each plant's natural form, growth habits, and maintenance needs rather than treating every variety the same.

Service Category 02

Seasonal Cleanup

Minnesota's changing seasons bring different property care needs. Our spring and fall cleanup services help keep outdoor areas maintained and ready for the months ahead.

Spring Services

Spring Cleanup

Refresh your property's appearance after winter with seasonal cleanup and grounds preparation.

Services may include clearing accumulated leaves and winter debris, cleaning landscape beds, and tidying maintained outdoor areas ahead of the growing season.

Request an Estimate
Autumn Services

Fall Cleanup

Keep your property looking cared for as autumn leaves fall and winter approaches.

Fall cleanup may include leaf collection, removal of seasonal yard debris, landscape bed cleanup, and general preparation of maintained outdoor areas for winter.

Cleanup and debris disposal: Depending on the property and approved scope, collected leaves and yard debris may be handled on-site or hauled away. Off-site removal and disposal are subject to availability and will be addressed in the estimate.

Service Category 03

Snow & Ice Management

Minnesota winters demand a practical approach to keeping properties accessible and maintaining outdoor travel areas.

OOS offers seasonal winter service arrangements as well as individual per-event services when scheduling and equipment availability permit.

Snow Services

Snow Plowing & Clearing

Snow plowing and clearing for appropriate driveways, parking areas, private roadways, and designated vehicle access areas.

Service arrangements are based on the property's layout, snow placement needs, and agreed maintenance requirements.

Request an Estimate
Pedestrian Access

Sidewalk & Walkway Snow Clearing

Snow clearing for sidewalks, pedestrian walkways, designated entryways, and other agreed access areas.

Appropriate hand tools and snow-clearing equipment are selected according to the property layout and required service.

Request an Estimate
Ice Management

Deicing & Ice Management

Help manage icy conditions on designated sidewalks, walkways, parking areas, and other appropriate outdoor surfaces.

Services may include applications of salt or suitable ice-melt products based on surface conditions, material suitability, and the agreed scope.

Winter service availability: Seasonal service contracts are available for qualifying properties. Individual per-event snow and ice services may also be available, depending on storm conditions, existing commitments, location, and scheduling.
